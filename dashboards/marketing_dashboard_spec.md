# Marketing Dashboard Specification

## Audience

Performance marketing, campaign managers and agencies.

## Primary decision

Which channels, campaigns, audiences and landing pages create quality demand?

## Required views

- Spend, impressions, clicks and sessions
- Web leads and qualified leads
- Cost per web lead and CPQL
- Campaign metadata compliance
- Landing page and model performance
- Channel quality matrix: volume versus quality
- Trend and anomaly annotations

## Warning

Attribution metrics are decision context; do not present them as causal proof without appropriate design.
