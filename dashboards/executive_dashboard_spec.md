# Executive Dashboard Specification

## Audience

Head of Digital and leadership.

## Primary decision

Where should the organisation invest, investigate or stop activity?

## Required sections

1. North Star trend: qualified leads per 1,000 engaged sessions.
2. Commercial progression: qualified leads, appointments, attendance, orders.
3. Channel quality: spend, CPQL and contribution.
4. Model performance: high-intent and qualified-lead progression.
5. Top opportunities and risks.
6. Data-quality status.
7. Active experiment status.

## Required insight card format

**Observation → likely driver → business impact → recommended action → owner.**
