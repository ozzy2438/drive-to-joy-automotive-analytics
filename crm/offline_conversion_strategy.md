# Offline Conversion Strategy

## Objective

Return approved CRM outcomes to the analytics warehouse so digital activity can be assessed against lead quality and sales progression.

## Outcome hierarchy

1. Web form submit
2. CRM matched lead
3. Qualified lead
4. Appointment booked
5. Appointment attended
6. Vehicle ordered
7. Vehicle delivered

## Reporting rule

Marketing channel reports should include both raw web conversions and downstream quality metrics. Optimisation should prioritise CPQL and qualified lead rate over cheap raw form volume.

## Attribution note

Downstream joins provide commercial context; they do not alone prove causal channel impact. Experiments or suitable causal methods are required for causal claims.
