# Lead Quality Definitions

| Category | Definition | Analytical treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified | Meets sales eligibility and intent rules | Primary CRM conversion |
| Duplicate | Existing or duplicate enquiry | Exclude from unique lead volume; monitor |
| Invalid | Contact details/record invalid | Quality issue |
| Uncontactable | Valid record but unable to reach | Operational quality metric |
| Out of Area | Not serviceable by dealer/network | Routing/coverage insight |
| Low Intent | Does not meet approved qualification | Nurture/research outcome |
| Existing Customer | May require a different journey | Segment separately |

Definitions must be agreed by CRM and sales stakeholders in a real implementation.
