# Dealer Handoff Strategy

## Objective

Measure whether digital demand is routed to a suitable dealer and progresses operationally.

## Required metrics

- Dealer selection rate
- Lead routing completeness
- Time to dealer contact
- Qualification rate by dealer
- Appointment booking and attendance by dealer
- Order progression by dealer
- Out-of-area rate
- Capacity flags where available

## Controls

Dealer IDs must be standardised, active/inactive status documented and state/metro-regional fields maintained in the reference dimension.
