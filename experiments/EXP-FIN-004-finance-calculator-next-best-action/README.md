# EXP-FIN-004 — Finance Calculator Next Best Action

## Problem

Users who complete repayment calculations may not know what to do next.

## Proposed treatment

Compare generic calculator completion page with a clear next-best action such as saved estimate, dealer selection or test-drive booking.

## Primary metric

Lead progression rate from finance-complete users.

## Guardrails

Calculator completion, form friction, lead quality and dealer capacity.

## Required next step

Create full pre-registration, calculation assumptions and tracking contract before launch.
