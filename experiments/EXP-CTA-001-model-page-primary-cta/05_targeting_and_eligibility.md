# Targeting and Eligibility

## Eligible users

- Visitors who view an eligible model page.
- Visitors with approved analytics consent where required by implementation.
- Anonymous and returning users who are not already in a conflicting experiment.

## Exclusions

- Internal traffic.
- Known test/developer users.
- Users with an active incompatible model-page experiment.
- Users who have already submitted a test-drive or quote form during the defined cooldown period.

## Randomisation

Use stable user/browser assignment with equal planned allocation across control, Treatment A and Treatment B.
