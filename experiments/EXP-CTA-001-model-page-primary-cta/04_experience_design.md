# Experience Design

| Variant | CTA | Destination | Intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Control | Book a Test Drive | Test-drive form | Direct sales action |

## Implementation controls

- Keep visual prominence comparable.
- Do not change unrelated page content during the test.
- Persist variant assignment for returning eligible visitors.
- Record `experiment_exposure` before CTA click and conversion events.
