# EXP-PERS-003 — Hybrid Researcher Personalisation

## Problem

Hybrid-model researchers may need ownership-cost and repayment reassurance rather than generic content.

## Proposed treatment

For eligible hybrid researchers, show a contextually relevant hybrid ownership-cost message and finance/configurator next-best action. Maintain a generic-experience holdout.

## Primary metric

Qualified lead rate or agreed high-intent progression metric.

## Guardrails

Experience performance, engagement overload, form quality, consent coverage and segment fairness.

## Required next step

Create full pre-registration and holdout specification before implementation.
