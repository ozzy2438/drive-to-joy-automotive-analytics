Connect automotive website behaviour to qualified CRM leads and downstream commercial outcomes while improving data integrity, customer experience and experimentation quality.

- GA4-style tracking, dataLayer and GTM governance

- A/B testing with sample planning, SRM and guardrails
- Personalisation audiences and holdout design
- Dashboard, Jira and Confluence operating model

### Primary metric

Qualified Leads per 1,000 Engaged Sessions.

All project-specific core data is synthetic. No proprietary Honda Australia data, systems or customer information is used.
