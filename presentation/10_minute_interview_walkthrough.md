# Ten-Minute Interview Walkthrough

## Minute 0–1: Context

Explain target role, fictional data policy and business problem.

## Minute 1–2: Journey

Walk model research → configuration/finance → dealer → form → CRM → qualified lead → attendance/order.

## Minute 2–3: Measurement

Show event catalogue, dataLayer contract, consent and web/CRM distinction.

## Minute 3–4: Architecture

Show Mermaid logical architecture and ADRs.

## Minute 4–5: Data model

Show raw/staging/intermediate/mart layers and lead funnel.

## Minute 5–6: Quality

Show critical checks, severity and runbooks.

## Minute 6–7: Dashboards

Show decision-specific dashboard specifications.

## Minute 7–8: Experimentation

Show EXP-CTA-001 package, sample size, SRM and guardrails.

## Minute 8–9: Personalisation

Show audience taxonomy and holdout strategy.

## Minute 9–10: Role alignment and limitations

Connect outputs to GA4/GTM, BigQuery/SQL/Python, dashboards, A/B testing, CRM/personalisation, stakeholders and Jira/Confluence. State that core data is synthetic and no Honda data was used.
