# Executive Case Study Outline

## Title

Drive to Joy — From Vehicle Research to Qualified Lead: An Automotive Digital Analytics Operating Model

## Narrative

1. Business problem: traffic and raw leads are not enough.
2. Customer journey: research to CRM and sales outcome.
3. Measurement: dataLayer, GTM, GA4-style events and consent.
4. Data platform: BigQuery-style warehouse and dbt marts.
5. Insight layer: executive, journey, marketing and quality reporting.
6. Optimisation: CTA experiment and form-friction testing.
7. Personalisation: audience and holdout strategy.
8. Operations: quality monitoring, runbooks, Jira/Confluence.
9. Limitations: synthetic data and responsible claims.
10. Role alignment: why this maps to Analytics Specialist work.
