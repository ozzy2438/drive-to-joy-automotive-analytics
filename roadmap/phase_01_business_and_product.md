# Phase 01 — Business and Product Definition

## Objective

Define the fictional automotive business problem, customer journey, success metrics and stakeholder needs before building technology.

## Required outputs

- Project charter
- Business context
- Role alignment
- Problem statement
- Scope
- Stakeholder map
- Success criteria
- Assumptions and constraints
- Risks and mitigations
- Personas
- Customer journey map
- Jobs to be done
- KPI tree
- Conversion definitions

## Tasks

1. Define the fictional automotive brand, vehicle range and dealer network.
2. Define six core customer personas.
3. Define the website-to-sales customer journey.
4. Define North Star, commercial, conversion, experience and quality metrics.
5. Define qualified-lead rules.
6. Define stakeholder decisions.
7. Define what cannot be claimed because the data is synthetic.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] Business problem is explicit.
- [ ] Journey is documented.
- [ ] KPI tree is complete.
- [ ] Qualified-lead definition is documented.
- [ ] Stakeholder decisions are identified.
- [ ] No claim implies use of real Honda data.
