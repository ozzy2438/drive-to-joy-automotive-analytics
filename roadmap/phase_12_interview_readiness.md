# Phase 12 — Interview Readiness

## Objective

Prepare role-specific evidence for the Honda Australia Analytics Specialist interview process.

## Required talking points

1. Why automotive journeys differ from generic ecommerce.
2. Why form submits are not qualified leads.
3. How web events connect to CRM and sales outcomes.
4. How dataLayer, GTM and GA4 work together.
5. Why BigQuery and dbt are used.
6. How data quality is monitored.
7. How A/B tests are designed and interpreted.
8. Why holdouts matter for personalisation.
9. How dashboards support decisions.
10. Project limitations.
11. What production implementation would require.
12. What the first 90 days in the target role would look like.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] Five-minute script rehearsed.
- [ ] Ten-minute walkthrough rehearsed.
- [ ] Role alignment clear.
- [ ] Limitations explainable.
- [ ] Technical choices defensible.
