# Phase 08 — Experimentation

## Objective

Implement a statistically responsible experimentation workflow.

## Required experiments

- `EXP-CTA-001` Model page primary CTA
- `EXP-FORM-002` Mobile form friction
- `EXP-PERS-003` Hybrid researcher personalisation
- `EXP-FIN-004` Finance calculator next-best action

## Workflow

1. Brief
2. Evidence
3. Hypothesis
4. Metrics
5. Power plan
6. Tracking design
7. QA
8. Launch
9. SRM and health monitoring
10. Analysis
11. Decision
12. Learning log

## Exit criteria

- [ ] At least one full experiment is documented.
- [ ] Exposure tracking works.
- [ ] Sample-size and SRM logic work.
- [ ] Guardrail metrics exist.
- [ ] Results are honestly labelled synthetic or live-demo.
