# Content Strategy

## Tone

Helpful, transparent, practical and customer-centred. Avoid claims that imitate Honda copy or branding.

## Content themes

- Vehicle fit and features
- Hybrid ownership considerations
- Finance clarity
- Dealer convenience
- Test-drive confidence
- Transparent next steps

## CTA guidance

Use intent-specific language:

- Build your vehicle
- Estimate your repayment
- Choose a nearby dealer
- Book a test drive
- Request a quote

Do not use misleading urgency or unsubstantiated savings claims.
