# Campaign Tagging Guide

## Required usage

Use the provided CSV template for all paid campaigns before launch.

## Rules

- Validate source, medium and campaign before campaign launch.
- Use approved lower-snake-case naming.
- Map every campaign to an owner, objective, vehicle/audience and landing page.
- Preserve campaign ID independently from human-readable naming.
- Audit missing or invalid UTMs daily/weekly depending on traffic.

## Examples

```text
utm_source=google
utm_medium=cpc
utm_campaign=au_search_lead_aurora_hybrid_launch_2026_07
```
