# KPI Tree

## North Star Metric

### Qualified Leads per 1,000 Engaged Sessions

```text
Qualified Leads per 1,000 Engaged Sessions =
(Qualified CRM Leads / Engaged Sessions) × 1,000
```

## KPI hierarchy

```text
Qualified Leads per 1,000 Engaged Sessions
├── Engaged Sessions
│   ├── Qualified traffic volume
│   ├── Landing-page relevance
│   ├── Site performance
│   └── Consent-aware coverage
└── Qualified Leads
    ├── Vehicle and configurator engagement
    ├── Finance and dealer progression
    ├── Form completion and error rate
    └── CRM qualification, attendance and order outcomes
```

## Core metrics

| Level | Metric | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| North Star | Qualified Leads per 1,000 Engaged Sessions | Qualified Leads / Engaged Sessions × 1,000 |
| Commercial | Cost per Qualified Lead | Media Spend / Qualified Leads |
| Commercial | Vehicle Order Rate | Vehicle Orders / Qualified Leads |
| Conversion | Test Drive Booking Rate | Test Drive Submits / Eligible Sessions |
| Experience | Configurator Completion Rate | Configurator Completions / Configurator Starts |
| Experience | Form Error Rate | Form Errors / Form Starts |
| Quality | CRM Match Rate | CRM-Matched Leads / Web Form Submits |
| Experiment | Incremental Qualified Lead Lift | Treatment QL Rate − Control QL Rate |

## Important rule

A form submit is a web conversion. A qualified lead is a CRM outcome. They must not be treated as equivalent.
