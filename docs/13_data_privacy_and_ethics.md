# Data Privacy and Ethics

## Data minimisation

Collect only the fields needed for documented measurement and business decisions.

## PII policy

Never send raw names, emails, phone numbers, addresses or payment data to GA4-style analytics tables. Use a generated and hashed lead key for approved warehouse matching.

## Consent policy

Track consent state as a measurement context. Do not interpret consent-denied traffic as fully sessionised or fully identifiable data.

## Fairness policy

Do not infer sensitive characteristics from browsing behaviour. Do not use protected or sensitive attributes for personalisation. Review audience rules for unequal treatment or harmful outcomes.

## Repository policy

All core data is synthetic. Public context sources must be documented with terms, intended use and privacy assessment.
