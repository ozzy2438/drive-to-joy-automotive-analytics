# Project Charter

## Project Name

Drive to Joy — Automotive Digital Analytics, Experimentation and Lead-Quality Intelligence Platform.

## Target Role

Honda Australia — Analytics Specialist.

## Purpose

Build a production-style analytics capability demonstrating ownership of digital measurement, reporting, customer-journey analysis, conversion optimisation, experimentation and personalisation in an automotive context.

## Primary Business Outcome

Increase qualified leads and vehicle-order contribution per engaged session while protecting customer experience and data integrity.

## Core Outcome

Enable stakeholders to understand which channels, campaigns, vehicle journeys, experiences and personalisation interventions create high-quality commercial outcomes.

## Primary Users

- Head of Digital
- Digital Experience Manager
- Performance Marketing Manager
- CRM and Lifecycle Marketing Manager
- Automotive Sales Operations Lead
- Dealer Network Operations Lead
- Product Owner
- UX Researcher
- Web Development Team
- Data and Analytics Team
- External Media and Technology Partners

## In Scope

- Automotive digital customer journey measurement
- Vehicle research and configurator tracking
- Finance-calculator tracking
- Dealer selection and lead handoff
- Test-drive and quote funnels
- CRM lead matching and qualification
- Channel and campaign performance
- Data-quality monitoring
- A/B experimentation
- Personalisation audience design
- Stakeholder reporting

## Out of Scope

- Honda proprietary systems or data
- Real vehicle sales or dealership operations
- Real customer PII
- Production Salesforce access
- Real campaign-budget management
- Claims of real Honda performance uplift

## Definition of Success

A technical implementer can use this repository to build a documented, demoable platform that demonstrates reliable measurement, data quality, BigQuery/dbt modelling, CRM closure, experimentation, personalisation and stakeholder decision support.
