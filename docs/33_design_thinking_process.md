# Design Thinking Process

## Discover

Use funnel analysis, device/channel segmentation, form errors, usability evidence, CRM disqualification reasons, dealer feedback and ethically implemented behavioural tools.

## Define

Write a precise customer problem, for example:

> Mobile hybrid-SUV researchers complete finance calculations but do not progress to test-drive booking; the likely barrier may be affordability uncertainty, form friction, CTA hierarchy or dealer-selection complexity.

## Ideate

Generate measurable interventions: CTA hierarchy, saved configuration, repayment explanation, shorter form, local availability, ownership-cost content or next-best action.

## Prototype and test

Test one clear change at a time with stated primary and guardrail metrics.

## Learn

Every outcome becomes rollout, iterate, stop, data-quality investigation or follow-up experiment.
