# Executive Reporting

## Weekly digital performance pack

### Required sections

1. Executive summary: three wins, three risks and three actions.
2. North Star trend: qualified leads per 1,000 engaged sessions.
3. Commercial performance: CPQL, appointments, attendance and order progression.
4. Channel quality: spend, leads, qualified leads and CPQL.
5. Journey health: model-to-form and form-to-CRM progression.
6. Data quality: freshness, match rate, event/UTM status.
7. Experiments: active tests, evidence status and decisions.
8. Actions: owner, due date, expected impact.

## Monthly business review

Include trend, cohort/segment observations, experiment learnings, dealer handoff patterns, data maturity progress and roadmap decisions.
