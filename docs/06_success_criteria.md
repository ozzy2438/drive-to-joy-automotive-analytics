# Success Criteria

## Product success

- A complete fictional automotive journey can be measured from model research to CRM lead outcome.
- Each stage has a documented event contract and business purpose.
- A demo website can generate consent-aware events.

## Data success

- Raw, staging, intermediate and mart layers are documented.
- dbt tests validate critical keys and business rules.
- CRM match, UTM, parameter completeness, anomaly and SRM checks are represented.

## Analytical success

- Dashboards answer defined stakeholder decisions.
- At least one complete experiment has a pre-registration and decision log.
- At least one personalisation audience includes a holdout group.

## Portfolio success

- A reviewer can understand the business problem, architecture, data policy and roadmap in five minutes.
- The repository clearly separates synthetic data from public context data.
- The project does not claim real Honda affiliation or commercial performance.
