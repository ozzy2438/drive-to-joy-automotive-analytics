# Problem Statement

## Core problem

The fictional automotive brand can observe traffic, page views and form submissions, but cannot reliably answer which channels, customer journeys and website experiences create qualified leads and downstream sales outcomes.

## Decision gaps

- Which marketing sources drive qualified rather than low-quality leads?
- Which models, variants and content journeys create strongest intent?
- Where do mobile and desktop users abandon?
- Does finance-calculator use help or hinder progression?
- Which CTA, form and personalised experiences create incremental gain?
- Can leadership trust event, UTM and CRM-match data?

## Consequences without solution

- Marketing optimises toward inexpensive low-quality lead volume.
- Digital teams improve engagement without commercial evidence.
- Dealer teams receive duplicates or uncontactable leads.
- Experiments are judged too early or on misleading metrics.
- Stakeholders lose confidence in reporting.

## Solution hypothesis

A governed platform that connects digital behaviour, campaign context, CRM status, experiment exposure and quality monitoring enables better customer-experience and commercial decisions.
