# Stakeholder Map

| Stakeholder | Decisions | Needs | Delivery cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Head of Digital | Investment and digital priorities | Qualified leads, cost efficiency, risks, opportunities | Weekly/monthly |
| Digital Experience Manager | Journey and feature priorities | Funnel friction, model engagement, CTA/form performance | Weekly |
| Performance Marketing Manager | Channel and campaign allocation | CPQL, landing-page quality, campaign hygiene | Daily/weekly |
| CRM/Lifecycle Manager | Nurture and personalisation | Audience eligibility, lead quality, holdout performance | Weekly |
| Sales Operations | Lead acceptance and dealer handoff | Match rate, qualification, contactability, attendance | Weekly |
| Dealer Operations | Local conversion and capacity | Dealer selection, booking, attendance and quality | Weekly |
| Product/UX | Experience improvements | Behaviour evidence, form errors, usability insights | Sprint cadence |
| Web Development | Tracking and release quality | dataLayer contract, QA criteria, incidents | Sprint/release |
| Analytics Engineering | Reliable models and data controls | Contracts, tests, freshness and lineage | Daily/weekly |
| Leadership | Strategic accountability | Concise commercial story and decisions | Monthly |

## Communication principle

Each output must name the decision owner, metric owner, data source, freshness expectation and recommended action.
