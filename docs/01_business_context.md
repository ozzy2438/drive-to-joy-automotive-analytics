# Business Context

## Fictional brand

**AstraDrive Australia** is a fictional direct-to-customer automotive brand used solely for this portfolio case study. It offers a simplified range of hybrid and petrol vehicles via a digital-first journey supported by a national dealer network.

## Business objective

Increase high-quality demand, not merely web traffic or raw form submissions. The primary commercial focus is improving the rate at which engaged visitors become CRM-qualified leads, attend test drives and progress to vehicle orders.

## Why automotive analytics differs

Automotive purchase journeys are long, multi-session, multi-device and frequently involve a handoff from web research to a dealer or sales team. A website analytics model must therefore distinguish discovery signals from sales-quality outcomes.

## Operating hypothesis

A governed analytics capability that joins website behaviour, campaign context, CRM outcomes and experimentation will help AstraDrive prioritise digital experiences that create meaningful commercial value.
