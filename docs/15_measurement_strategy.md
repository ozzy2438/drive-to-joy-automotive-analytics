# Measurement Strategy

## Measurement principle

Measure meaningful journey progress and commercial outcomes rather than vanity metrics alone.

## Journey stages

| Stage | Main customer intent | Key events |
|---|---|---|
| Discover | Find relevant vehicle range | landing and range views |
| Research | Evaluate model and specifications | model, variant, comparison, offer views |
| Configure | Build a preferred vehicle | configurator start/steps/complete |
| Evaluate | Understand affordability and local availability | finance calculator, dealer search/select |
| Convert | Request sales engagement | form starts, errors and submits |
| Progress | Move through sales lifecycle | CRM qualification, attendance, order |

## Measurement hierarchy

- Micro-conversions: research, comparison, configuration and finance actions.
- Primary web conversions: test-drive and quote submits.
- CRM conversions: qualified lead and appointment outcomes.
- Commercial outcomes: vehicle order and delivery where available.

## Reliability requirements

Every report must show freshness and quality status. Every conversion definition must name its source system and known limitations.
