# Alerting Strategy

| Severity | Channel | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Immediate owner notification and report annotation | Investigate immediately |
| High | Team channel/ticket | Investigate within one business day |
| Medium | Backlog/weekly quality review | Plan remediation |
| Low | Governance maintenance list | Fix opportunistically |

Alerts must include check name, affected period, likely impact, owner and runbook link.
