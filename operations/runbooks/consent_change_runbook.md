# Consent Change Runbook

## Trigger

Material consent-rate shift after CMP/content/release change.

## Actions

1. Confirm CMP version/release timing.
2. Compare device/channel/page mix.
3. Check event/session coverage changes.
4. Annotate trend and experiment analysis.
5. Engage privacy/web owner for review.
6. Do not interpret affected funnel changes without caveat.
