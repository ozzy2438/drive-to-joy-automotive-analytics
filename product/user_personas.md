# User Personas

## Hybrid Family Researcher

Needs practical space, ownership clarity and trustworthy comparison. Likely friction: finance complexity and too many competing CTAs.

## Urban Professional

Needs rapid mobile research and easy booking. Likely friction: time, form length and unclear model differences.

## Returning Configurator User

Needs continuity. Likely friction: lost progress and generic return experience.

## Finance-Anxious Buyer

Needs transparent illustrative repayment explanation. Likely friction: uncertainty rather than lack of product interest.

## Regional Buyer

Needs local dealer and availability confidence. Likely friction: travel/delivery uncertainty.

## Existing Owner Upgrade Candidate

Needs relevant upgrade/trade-in path. Requires approved consented CRM context in real implementation.
