# Customer Journey Map

| Stage | Customer goal | Behaviour | Friction | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Discover | Find relevant vehicle | Search/landing/range | Generic landing relevance | Channel/landing alignment |
| Research | Understand fit | Model/spec/compare | Complex information | Better hierarchy/content |
| Configure | Build preference | Configurator | Lost progress/complexity | Resume and simplify |
| Evaluate | Understand affordability | Finance calculator | Uncertainty | Next-best action |
| Convert | Contact sales | Dealer/form | Form and routing friction | Shorter guided flow |
| Progress | Complete purchase journey | CRM/dealer | Follow-up inconsistency | Lead-quality closure |
