# Usability Testing Plan

## Goal

Identify friction in model research, configuration, finance evaluation, dealer selection and form completion.

## Tasks

1. Find a suitable hybrid SUV.
2. Compare two models.
3. Build a preferred configuration.
4. Estimate a repayment.
5. Find a nearby dealer.
6. Request a test drive.

## Measures

Task completion, time, confusion points, error recovery, perceived confidence and qualitative feedback.

## Privacy

Use consented participants and do not publish identifiable recordings.
